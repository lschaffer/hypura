Is it a problem with Hypura / the Model?
Yes. Smaller or local models (SLMs like 3B–8B) commonly have an "over-completion" bias. When they see a list of device IDs in the JSON context, they reflexively want to iterate over each ID and fetch individual details, even when they already have all the required data.
